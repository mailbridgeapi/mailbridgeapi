# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Report it privately through the support center at <https://dashboard.mailbridgeapi.com/support/> and mark the ticket as a security report.

Include the affected endpoint, a clear impact description, safe reproduction steps, and the UTC time of any test. Do not include real mailbox passwords, API keys, Google tokens, customer messages, or unrelated personal data.

## Safe testing

Test only accounts and mailboxes you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Do not disrupt the service, access another customer account, send unsolicited mail, or retain data obtained during testing.
